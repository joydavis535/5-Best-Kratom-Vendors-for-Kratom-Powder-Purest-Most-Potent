## How to Store Kratom Powder Properly

**Airtight container** — Oxygen exposure oxidizes mitragynine. Minimize headspace. Mason jars with tight lids work well.

**Dark location** — UV exposure accelerates alkaloid degradation. Store in cabinet, drawer, or opaque container. Never on a countertop in direct light.

**Room temperature or below** — Heat accelerates breakdown. Cool, room-temperature storage is adequate. Cooler extends shelf life further.

**Away from moisture** — Moisture promotes mold growth and alkaloid degradation. Never in bathrooms or humid environments. Use silica gel moisture-absorbing packets in storage containers.

Properly stored powder from quality vendors maintains potency for **12–24 months** from production. Poorly stored powder degrades significantly within 3–6 months.
